About

Curiosity isthe through line.

I’ve always been drawn to the questions behind the design.

I began in visual communication, creating websites and email campaigns. Over time, I became more interested in the questions that came before the design: why this experience, who it serves, and what it could make possible.

That curiosity led me to VCU Brandcenter, where I studied business and experience design, then to frog and Happy Money. My work has grown to include healthcare concepts, enterprise platforms, borrower experiences, and the systems that help teams design with greater consistency.

Across those settings, I’ve found the most meaning in connecting the pieces. Research and direction. Customer needs and business priorities. The experience we imagine and the realities of delivering it.

I want to keep growing where those connections are made, helping shape product direction and leading design from the first question through the finished experience.
